Where the funnel is winning or leaking

actuals for the period, against your own benchmark average
How to read the two columns. The left figure is the selected period. The right is the KPI benchmark: for volume metrics (reach, impressions, clicks, leads, thruplays) it is the average per week over the benchmark range; for rate and cost metrics (CTR, CPM, CPC, CPL, CTL, frequency) it is the aggregate ratio across the whole benchmark — never the average of daily ratios, which skews badly when spend is uneven. Green always means better: for costs that is down. The arrow on a volume metric compares the period total against what the benchmark's weekly rate would have produced over the same number of weeks, so it stays like-for-like whatever period you pick.
