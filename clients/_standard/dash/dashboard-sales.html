How to read the two columns. The left figure is the selected period. The right is the KPI benchmark: for volume metrics (revenue, orders, customers, spend) it is the average per week over the benchmark range; for rate metrics (AOV, ROAS, CTR, CPM) it is the aggregate ratio across the whole benchmark — never the average of daily ratios. The arrow on a volume metric compares the period total against what the benchmark's weekly rate would have produced over the same number of weeks.

Why the channel names look the way they do
Every referrer — order attribution and session attribution alike — goes through one normaliser in the export job. That is the only reason a visitors-against-buyers comparison is valid. The store's own second domain and on-site apps collapse to direct; a substring match on the shop handle is not enough, because punctuation differs between the handle and the domain.
