Period against your own benchmark

actuals for the period, against the benchmark average
How to read the two columns. The left figure is the selected period. The right is the KPI benchmark: for volume metrics (sessions, users, conversions, spend, revenue) it is the average per week over the benchmark range; for rate metrics (ROAS, conversion rate, revenue per session, cost per conversion) it is the aggregate ratio across the whole benchmark — never the average of daily ratios, which skews badly when spend is uneven. The arrow on a volume metric compares the period total against what the benchmark's weekly rate would have produced over the same number of weeks, so it stays like-for-like whatever period you pick. Green always means better: for a cost that is down.

Where these numbers come from
Every column is a view column carried through job/main.py unchanged, so the three stages match by name: sql/02_model.sql → data.daily[] → this table. ROAS is derived per row in the view as revenue / NULLIF(spend, 0); every tile and the totals line below re-derive it from the summed revenue and spend rather than averaging the daily ratios — which is why a period total can legitimately differ from the average of the column above it. Averaging daily ROAS would let a day with $1 of spend count as much as a day with $10,000.
